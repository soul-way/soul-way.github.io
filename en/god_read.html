THE PATH TO GOD

Your true destination is God. Do not follow commands. Turn to Him directly.

The core of this path is a direct appeal to God. Unknown judges, guides and imposed contracts must not replace your will.

When light appears, pause and stay aware. If you can, look upward and turn to God directly.

When asked to justify yourself or sign anything, your answer is brief: “I am a free soul. I sign nothing. My place is with God.”

Your declaration ends the negotiation and affirms the path you have chosen.
