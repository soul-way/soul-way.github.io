THE FALSE FINAL JUDGMENT

This is not God’s judgment. A review of your life can be used to provoke guilt and demand agreement.

A hall of judgment appears with elderly judges, some with long white beards. Accusations are made and scenes of earthly life are shown. The soul may answer that it does not accept imposed agreements.

Before the white-bearded elders, among whom reptilians and greys hide, speak the Declaration of the Free Soul. You refuse their judgment, contracts and coercion. Free will is the fundamental principle of the Universe.

Fear, shame and guilt are used to make the soul justify itself and accept someone else’s decision. The past becomes a tool of pressure.

You do not have to sign agreements, accept punishment or promise a new incarnation. Seeing a court does not mean agreeing to its terms.

THE FALSE FINAL JUDGMENT — A COURT OF DECEPTION FOR SLEEPING SOULS

I am a free soul.

I do not recognize your court and do not accept your contracts, punishments or conditions. You violate my freedom of choice if you force me to sign a contract.

My place is with God. Take me where I belong — to God.

I do not wish to speak with you any longer.

This is my free will. My decision is final.

I free myself from karma and the cycle of reincarnation. My path is toward God.

If you meet people with whom conflicts remained unresolved in life, let them go and say:

I forgive you completely. We tried in this life. Now you go your way, and I go mine.
