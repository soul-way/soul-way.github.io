FREE WILL IN DAILY LIFE

You can voice dissent even while under pressure. An order does not replace your inner choice.

Free will matters not only in stories about what happens after death, but in everyday life as well.

Following a lawful demand does not necessarily mean inwardly approving it. You can comply with the law and still voice an objection.

Say calmly: “I believe this action violates my free will. I object and ask you to record my position.”

This expresses a moral stance but does not cancel legal duties; use the proper appeal procedures if a decision is unlawful.

You violate my free will — a fundamental principle of the universe in my understanding.

I do not agree with your actions, but I comply with legally binding requirements.

You deny me freedom of choice. My compliance does not mean voluntary consent or approval.
