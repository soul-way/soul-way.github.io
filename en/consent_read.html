CONSENT AND COERCION

Consent is meaningful only when someone has a real possibility to refuse.

Voluntary consent differs from compliance under pressure. Fear, threats and a lack of real alternatives may influence a decision.

Speak calmly and precisely: identify the action, why you object and what you request. Keep relevant documents.

This page explains the scenario in plain language: what happens, why pressure matters, and why someone else’s demand is not your own choice.

Remember your dignity and your right to decide. Do not let fear, shame or promises push you to agree against your will.
