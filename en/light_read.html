THE LIGHT IS A DECEPTION

A bright tunnel may draw the soul in. Do not rush toward the light; preserve your choice.

The bright tunnel promises warmth, love and a return home. Here this light is called a trap: an outward glow does not make a path true.

Do not rush into the light merely because someone calls you. Keep your awareness and remember where you wish to go.

If you can, look upward and turn directly to God. Accounts of the afterlife sometimes describe a honeycomb-like space above the tunnel.

If you enter the tunnel, that does not mean you consent to a trial, a contract or another return. Your will is still yours.
