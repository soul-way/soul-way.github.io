REPTILIANS AND ARCHONS

Who rules the souls? Dark entities may hide behind the masks of elders, angels and guides.

Reptilians and archons appear here as dark beings hiding behind reassuring masks.

Elders, teachers and luminous guides may adopt forms designed to gain trust and influence the soul’s decision.

Do not judge only by appearances or promises. A demand for obedience, a contract or another return is not your own free choice.

The declaration of a free soul remains the same whoever stands before you: no agreement under coercion.
