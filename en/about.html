Traps, free choice and the path to God — where to begin.

Read the full article →

This website helps newcomers understand accounts of traps on Earth and after death. It brings together themes of light and the tunnel, meeting figures, the Council and judgment, soul contracts, karma, reincarnation and free will.

The central point is simple: no one should demand your consent through threats, punishment or guilt. In everyday life this means knowing your rights, being able to say no, and distinguishing lawful requirements from pressure.

Some regression accounts describe a luminous tunnel after death. They portray it both as a route and as a possible way of directing consciousness toward guides and judgment. The site encourages readers not to act automatically out of fear or habit.
