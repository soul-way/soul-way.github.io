Short answers to the most common questions visitors ask about the light, the beings who meet the soul, false judgment, contracts and the path to God.

1. What happens right after death?

Consciousness continues to perceive what is happening. A new stage opens before the soul, and it may include light, meeting figures, guides and attempts to direct the soul’s choice.

2. Why should you not go into the white light automatically?

The bright light works as a lure. It creates trust, calms the soul and pushes it to follow without an independent decision. Stop first, gather yourself and turn upward to God.

3. Who can meet the soul?

The soul may be met by images of relatives, angels, elders, guides and other beings. A familiar appearance is used to create trust. No image should be accepted as true just because it looks kind.

4. What is false judgment?

It is a staged scene where the soul is shown its life, mistakes, pain and weaknesses to trigger guilt, fear and consent to a new form of submission. Such a judgment has no rightful authority over the soul.

5. Should you justify yourself or sign anything?

No. Do not argue, do not sign a contract, do not agree to punishment and do not consent to a new incarnation. State your choice clearly and end the exchange.

6. What should you say in the decisive moment?

I am a free soul. I do not recognize your court and do not accept your contracts, punishments or conditions. You violate my freedom of choice if you force me to sign a contract. My place is with God. Take me where I belong — to God. I do not wish to speak with you any longer. This is my free will. My decision is final.
