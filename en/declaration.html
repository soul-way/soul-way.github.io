DECLARATION OF THE FREE SOUL

You must speak this declaration at the false Final Judgment, when judges appear as white-bearded elders, angels and guides. In this afterlife narrative, dark reptilians, greys, the devil and other beings hide behind those masks and demand obedience.

These words are to be spoken at the Final Judgment, when others try to impose contracts, punishments, or another reincarnation on you.

If after death you find yourself before a court of white-bearded elders, angels, spiritual teachers, or other beings, do not rush to acknowledge their authority.

Dark forces — reptilians, greys, devil-like beings, and other deceivers — may hide behind the appearance of kind elders and beings of light. Their appearance is not a reason to grant consent.

They may show you scenes from your earthly life, remind you of mistakes, stir up guilt, and insist that you must accept punishment, sign a contract, or be born again on Earth.

At this moment, speak the Declaration of the Free Soul calmly, firmly, and without fear.

Free will is the fundamental principle on which this spiritual declaration is based. No one has the right to demand your consent through threats, deception, or coercion.

If the judges keep insisting after your refusal, that is an attempt to violate your freedom of choice. Coercion does not turn refusal into consent.

No imposed contract should determine your will. At the same time, it cannot be reliably guaranteed that saying the declaration will, by itself, change what happens after death.

Speak the following words:

I am a free soul.

I do not recognize your court.

I do not accept your contracts, punishments, or conditions.

You violate my freedom of choice when you try to make me sign a contract or decide against my will.

My place is with God. Take me where I belong — to God.

I do not wish to speak with you any longer.

This is my free will. My decision is final.
