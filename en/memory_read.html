THE SOUL’S MEMORIES

Your entire life is played back, and guilt is used as pressure. Feeling guilty is not the same as giving consent.

At the false judgment, scenes of your earthly life may appear: actions, words, mistakes and painful memories. Guilt and shame receive special attention.

The purpose of this scene is to make the soul feel unworthy and seek permission from unknown judges.

Taking responsibility for actions is not the same as consenting to someone else’s punishment, contract or reincarnation.

Remember: another figure’s reading of your memories cannot replace your free will.
