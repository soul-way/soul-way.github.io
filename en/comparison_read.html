WHAT DO REGRESSION STORIES SAY?

Different accounts repeat themes of light, guides, a council, memories and reincarnation.

Similar images recur in different stories: light, elders, relatives, a council room and a life review. The details and interpretations differ between authors.

Compare accounts without confusing the stories with interpretations. Original channels are collected in the source library.

This page explains the scenario in plain language: what happens, why pressure matters, and why someone else’s demand is not your own choice.

Remember your dignity and your right to decide. Do not let fear, shame or promises push you to agree against your will.
