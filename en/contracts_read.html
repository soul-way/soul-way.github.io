SOUL CONTRACTS

You may be offered another agreement, mission or incarnation. You are a free soul. Sign nothing.

A soul contract is an agreement that may be offered by guides or during the false judgment.

Past mistakes, karmic debts and urgent missions may be used as reasons to accept another return.

Do not decide out of fear and do not sign imposed terms. Even a promise of forgiveness or reward does not remove your right to refuse.

Your response can be short: “I am a free soul. I will not enter any contracts or agreements with you.”
